Finding and segmenting subscribers

The subscriber search box accepts simple conditions, so you can target exactly who you mean.

Examples

Type thisFinds
subscribers.name LIKE 'Anna%'Everyone whose name starts with Anna
subscribers.email LIKE '%@gmail.com'All Gmail addresses
subscribers.attribs->>'city' = 'London'Subscribers whose stored city is London
subscribers.created_at > NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days'Joined in the last 30 days

Combine conditions with AND / OR. After searching you can select the results and send a campaign to just that segment.
