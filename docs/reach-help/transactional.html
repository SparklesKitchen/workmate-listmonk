Transactional e-mails

Transactional templates are for one-to-one automated e-mails: welcome messages, receipts, confirmations. They are sent to a single subscriber, not to a list.

Creating one

Under Campaigns → Templates → New, choose type Transactional. Give it a subject and body.

Personalisation codes

CodeWhat it prints
{{ .Subscriber.Name }}Full name
{{ .Subscriber.FirstName }} / {{ .Subscriber.LastName }}First / last name
{{ .Subscriber.Email }}E-mail address
{{ .Subscriber.Attribs.city }}Any custom attribute you store (here: city)
{{ .Tx.Data.order_id }}Any value passed when the e-mail is triggered (here: order_id)

Do I need this?

Only if something in your business should trigger automatic one-to-one e-mails. For regular newsletters, campaign templates are all you need.
