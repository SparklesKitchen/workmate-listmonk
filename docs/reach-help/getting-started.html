Getting started

1. Your list

A list is your audience. Subscribers join it through your signup form and you send campaigns to it.

2. Get subscribers

Under Subscribers → Import upload existing contacts, or put your signup form on your website: Lists → Forms, pick your list, and use the form designer to generate the embed code.

3. Set up delivery

Under Delivery, connect your sending provider and verify your sender address. Nothing can send until this is done.

4. Send a campaign

Under Campaigns → Create new: name, subject, pick your list, write your content, send yourself a test, then send.
